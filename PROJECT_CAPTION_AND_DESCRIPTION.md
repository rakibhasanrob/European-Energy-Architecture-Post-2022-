> • **Southern Corridors:** Caspian gas via the **Trans-Adriatic Pipeline (TAP)** from Azerbaijan and expanded **TransMed** flows from Algeria anchored Southern Europe.

### Project Overview: European Energy Architecture (2022 Transformation)
**Objective:** Visualize the structural dissolution of Eurasian pipeline dependency and Europe's rapid infrastructural pivot toward Atlantic maritime LNG and alternative regional corridors following the 2022 Ukraine invasion.

### Cartographic & Editorial Narrative:
1. **Phase 1: Pre-2022 Eurasian Interdependence**
   The first map illustrates the historical architecture established over half a century. Vast Siberian gas basins funnel westward through three primary overland and subsea conduits into Central European off-takers. European heavy industry and chemical hubs (e.g., PCK Schwedt in Germany, CEGH Baumgarten in Austria) were geographically engineered around fixed Urals crude and Siberian methane pipelines.

2. **Phase 2: Post-2022 Structural Realignment**
   The second map visualizes the immediate decoupling. Key pipeline arteries are depicted as severed or terminated (Nord Stream rupture, Yamal stoppage, Druzhba North shutdown). To compensate for the loss of over 100 bcm of baseline capacity, the map highlights the emergence of the "Global LNG Bridge"—a network of coastal regasification terminals and floating storage units (FSRUs) receiving transatlantic carrier fleets, paired with enhanced flow vectors from Norway (Baltic Pipe), the Caspian Basin (Southern Gas Corridor/TAP), and North Africa (TransMed).

### Cartographic Design Choices:
- **Dark Minimalist Basemap:** Powered by CartoDB Dark Matter, suppressing municipal clutter while preserving national contours and geographical context.
- **Color Semantics:** Glowing neon lines represent active energy streams; dashed crimson vectors signify severed or sabotaged infrastructure; soft thematic polygon fills distinguish sanctioning consumer blocs from alternative suppliers.
- **High-Resolution Rendering:** Dual WebGL canvas and DOM rasterization producing crisp 4K/retina static visual exports.
