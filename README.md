# European-Energy-Architecture-Post-2022-
A comparative geospatial visualization examining the dissolution of Eurasian pipeline interdependence following the 2022 Ukraine invasion, and Europe's rapid structural pivot toward Atlantic maritime LNG and alternative regional corridors.

Key Insights:

•	Pre-2022: Depicts the historical reliance on Siberian gas basins channeled across the Baltic Sea and overland through Ukraine and Belarus directly into Germany and Central European refining hubs.

•	Post-2022: Maps the severed corridors (Nord Stream explosions, Yamal cessation, Druzhba North embargo) against the newly established coastal FSRU network, the Baltic Pipe from Norway, and Southern Gas Corridor flows from Azerbaijan.
